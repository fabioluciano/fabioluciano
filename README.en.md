My professional journey is marked by a dedication to transforming complex challenges into scalable and efficient solutions, always with a keen eye on innovation and operational sustainability. I am an open-source enthusiast, maintaining popular projects like `tmux-powerkit`, and a relentless explorer of new technologies.

My expertise spans from **Cloud Architecture & FinOps** to **Platform Engineering (IDP)**, with a strong focus on **Developer Experience (DevEx)**, **Modern DevOps & CI/CD**, **DevSecOps & Security**, **Software Engineering**, **Kubernetes & Containers**, and **Observability & SRE**. With a solid foundation in Go, Rust, TypeScript, and Python, and extensive experience in ecosystems like Kubernetes, Linux, and CLI tools, I continuously seek to expand my horizons.
