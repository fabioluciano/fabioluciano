## 👋 About Me
Hello! I'm Fábio Luciano, a Solutions Architect with a deep passion for transforming how engineering teams build and deliver software. My professional journey focuses on optimizing Developer Experience (DevEx), ensuring developers work in efficient and inspiring environments. With a solid foundation in Software Engineering, I dedicate myself to building resilient and scalable systems, always with a keen eye for innovation and performance.

My expertise spans from **Cloud Architecture & FinOps**, where I seek solutions that balance performance and cost, to implementing **Modern DevOps & CI/CD** to accelerate the software development lifecycle. I am a strong advocate for **Kubernetes & Containers**, using them to orchestrate complex applications, and a firm believer in **DevSecOps & Security**, integrating security practices from design to deployment. Furthermore, I actively engage in **Platform Engineering (IDP)**, creating tools and infrastructures that empower other teams, and I am an expert in **Observability & SRE**, ensuring the continuous reliability and performance of systems. My curiosity drives me to constantly explore new technologies and patterns, as evidenced by my vast collection of starred projects and engagement with the open-source community.
