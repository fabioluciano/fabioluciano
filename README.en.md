## 👋 About Me
I'm Fábio Luciano, a Solutions Architect and Platform Engineer passionate about transforming how development teams work. With extensive experience in infrastructure and software, my primary focus is to design and implement solutions that not only function but also empower developers, optimize costs, and ensure security and scalability.

My expertise spans from **Cloud Architecture & FinOps**, ensuring infrastructures are efficient and cost-effective, to **Platform Engineering (IDP)**, where I build the tools and environments that accelerate the development lifecycle. I am a tireless advocate for **Developer Experience (DevEx)**, believing that a friction-free workflow is key to team productivity and satisfaction.

I am deeply involved in **DevOps & Modern CI/CD**, automating delivery processes, and in **DevSecOps & Security**, integrating security practices from design to operation. My passion for systems and performance leads me to explore **Software Engineering** with languages like Go, Rust, and TypeScript, always seeking robust and elegant solutions. Furthermore, I specialize in **Kubernetes & Containers** for workload orchestration and in **Observability & SRE**, ensuring the resilience and health of critical systems.

Currently, I am dedicated to deepening and applying my knowledge in the following areas, aiming to build more efficient and secure development ecosystems:

*   ☁️ **Cloud Architecture & FinOps**: Designing and optimizing cloud infrastructures for scalability, resilience, and cost control, ensuring that cloud investments yield maximum returns.

*   ☸️ **Kubernetes & Containers**: Orchestrating applications in containerized environments to ensure high availability, automatic scalability, and simplified management of microservices.
*   📊 **Observability & SRE**: Ensuring system health and performance through proactive monitoring, structured logging, distributed tracing, and the implementation of Site Reliability Engineering practices.
