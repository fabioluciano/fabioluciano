## 👋 About Me

Hello! I'm Fábio Luciano, a Solutions Architect with a deep passion for Platform Engineering and optimizing Developer Experience (DevEx). With a career focused on building and optimizing technological ecosystems, I operate at the intersection of complex infrastructure challenges and the need for productive, engaged development teams.

My expertise spans a broad spectrum of disciplines, including **Cloud Architecture & FinOps**, where I seek efficient and economically viable solutions; **Developer Experience (DevEx)**, aiming to simplify developers' workflows; **DevOps & Modern CI/CD**, automating and accelerating the software lifecycle; and **DevSecOps & Security**, ensuring security is a pillar from inception. I am also an expert in **Platform Engineering (IDP)**, creating robust internal developer platforms; **Software Engineering**, applying sound principles for scalable systems; **Kubernetes & Containers**, mastering large-scale container orchestration; and **Observability & SRE**, ensuring the reliability and performance of distributed systems.

While my public commit activity might be lighter, my focus extends to architecture, mentorship, code review, and contributions to internal and private projects. I am a constant explorer of new technologies, with a particular interest in open source, where I maintain projects like the popular `tmux-powerkit`, and I'm always looking for ways to improve how we build and deliver software.

## 🎯 Current Focus & Interests

Currently, I am deeply involved in and exploring the following areas, always seeking best practices and innovations:

*   ☁️ **Cloud Architecture & FinOps**: Designing and optimizing cloud infrastructures for maximum efficiency, scalability, and cost control, with a focus on platforms like AWS and Azure.
*   👨‍💻 **Developer Experience (DevEx)**: Creating tools, automations, and processes that empower developers, reducing friction and increasing productivity and satisfaction in the development cycle.
*   🔄 **DevOps & Modern CI/CD**: Implementing end-to-end continuous integration and continuous delivery pipelines, using tools like Tekton, Jenkins, and GitLab CI to automate and accelerate deployments.
*   🔐 **DevSecOps & Security**: Integrating security as an intrinsic part of the development pipeline, from static code analysis (SAST) to runtime vulnerability management.
*   🏗️ **Platform Engineering (IDP)**: Developing and maintaining internal developer platforms that abstract infrastructure complexity, allowing teams to focus on business logic.
*   ⚙️ **Software Engineering**: Applying software design principles, distributed architectures, and clean code patterns to build robust, maintainable, and high-performance systems.
*   ☸️ **Kubernetes & Containers**: Orchestrating and managing containerized applications at scale, exploring ecosystems like Helm, ArgoCD, and operators for advanced automation.
*   📊 **Observability & SRE**: Implementing monitoring, logging, and tracing solutions (with Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry) to ensure the health, reliability, and performance of systems in production.
