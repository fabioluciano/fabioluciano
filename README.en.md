## 👋 About Me
Hello! I'm Fábio Luciano, a Solutions Architect passionate about building robust, scalable, and efficient systems. With a solid background in software engineering, I am dedicated to optimizing the developer experience and ensuring the reliability and performance of complex infrastructures.

My expertise deeply covers areas such as **Cloud Architecture & FinOps**, **Developer Experience (DevEx)**, **Modern DevOps & CI/CD**, **DevSecOps & Security**, **Platform Engineering (IDP)**, **Software Engineering**, **Kubernetes & Containers**, and **Observability & SRE**. Through these disciplines, I constantly seek to innovate and implement solutions that boost productivity and security in development and production environments.

I am an open-source enthusiast, a maintainer of projects like `tmux-powerkit`, and always exploring new technologies and methodologies. I believe that continuous learning and collaboration are pillars for technical excellence, and I share my thoughts and discoveries on my personal blog.
